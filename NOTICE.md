# Notices

## Icon provenance - check before publishing

Many line icons in `icons/ui/` use the same geometry as the Feather (MIT) and Lucide (ISC) icon sets, for example
`truck`, `users`, `shopping-cart`, `activity` and `navigation`. Treat the whole `ui/` set as derived from them unless
you know a given icon is entirely your own drawing.

Both licences allow reuse in an AGPL-3.0 project, but they require you to keep their copyright and permission
notice. Before the repository goes public, paste those two notices into this file:

- Feather: Copyright (c) Cole Bemis, MIT licence - https://github.com/feathericons/feather
- Lucide: ISC licence (plus the Feather MIT notice for the parts inherited from it) - https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide

## Brand logos

The glyphs in `icons/social/` (and any other brand marks) are trademarks of their owners. They are included so
projects can link to those services; the AGPL does not give anyone rights to the marks, and `google.svg` keeps
Google's own colours. Follow each brand's usage guidelines. Where a brand forbids redistribution of its logo,
remove it from the repository and keep it in your private project instead.

## Project-specific artwork

`icons/other/` holds artwork made for individual projects (store favicon, image placeholder). They are not general
purpose icons; move them to the owning project if you do not want them licensed under the AGPL.
